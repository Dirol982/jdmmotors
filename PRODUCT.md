# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, published with GitHub Pages from this repository (delegated: Pages hosting needs plain static files).

## Users
Owners of Toyota and Lexus cars in Minsk and Belarus: daily drivers due for service, owners of cars imported from the USA, Canada and China who need russification and adaptation, and buyers checking a car before purchase. They arrive from search or Instagram, usually on a phone, and want to know quickly whether this shop can fix their car and how to book.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for JDM Motors, an independent service station (СТО) specialising only in Toyota and Lexus. Success is a call or a booking request for a service.

## Positioning
Single-brand specialists: only Toyota and Lexus since 2010, masters certified by Toyota (Team21 Diagnosis Master Technician, Pro Technician, Toyota Technician), official partners of Toyota Custom Team. Rare competence in russification of Toyota from China and Lexus from the USA/Canada, adaptation of US cars, navigation updates.

## Operating Context
Workshop at Минск, Брилевский тупик, д.16, к.3, with a graffiti "JDM" wall facing the yard. Mon–Fri 9:00–19:00, urgent repair on weekends. Booking by phone or a request form (date, time, type of work, parts selection yes/no).

## Capabilities and Constraints
Services: diagnostics, engine, body repair and paint (all makes), maintenance "всё включено", oil and fluids, suspension, steering, brakes, fuel system, exhaust, auto electrics, equipment installation, pre-purchase check, parts sales, russification (Toyota China/USA/Canada, Lexus USA/Canada), adaptation of US cars, Starline alarms, autostart from stock key, navigation updates, Android navigation, chip tuning, camera washers, power liftgate, soft-close doors, rear-seat monitors.
Offer on the current site: diagnostics for 49 BYN (suspension, brakes, battery, fluids, computer diagnostics). Claim: up to 40% cheaper than the dealer without losing warranty. Masters with 10+ years of experience.
The static site has no backend: the booking form must hand off to phone/messenger, not pretend to submit to a server.

## Evidence on Hand
Real workshop photos, certificates and staff portraits downloaded from jdmmotors.by into assets/img. Rating on the current site: 4.8 from 330 reviews; Yandex Maps shows 5.0 (≈435 ratings). No verbatim customer reviews were collected: do not invent quotes. No price list beyond the 49 BYN diagnostics offer.

## Product Principles
- Specialisation is the proof: everything says Toyota and Lexus only.
- Show the real workshop and real people, never stock.
- Every screen offers a one-tap call.
- Facts over adjectives: years, certificates, ratings, prices that exist.
